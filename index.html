3D Pong

Three.js pong — play local split-screen, or open a room across two devices.

online

Host takes the left paddle, guest the right. Move with W/S or ↑/↓. Create Room shows a short code to type — works from any computer or phone, no camera needed. It also puts a QR code up if you'd rather just point a phone at it. Play is browser-to-browser; you both need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
